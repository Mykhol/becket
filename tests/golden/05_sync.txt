### sync an up-to-date workspace (clean no-op rebase onto origin/main)

$ becket sync ws
[exit 0]
--- stdout ---
▸ Syncing service ← origin/main...
Current branch feature/ws is up to date.

▸ Sync complete.
--- stderr ---
From <SANDBOX>/origins/service
 * branch            main       -> FETCH_HEAD

### sync picks up new commits on the base branch

$ becket sync ws
[exit 0]
--- stdout ---
▸ Syncing service ← origin/main...

▸ Sync complete.
--- stderr ---
From <SANDBOX>/origins/service
 * branch            main       -> FETCH_HEAD
Successfully rebased and updated refs/heads/feature/ws.

# worktrees: service
<SANDBOX>/platform/.becket/workspaces/ws/service  <SHA> [feature/ws]
<SANDBOX>/platform/service                        <SHA> [main]

### sync refuses a stacked workspace and points at restack

$ becket sync child
[exit 1]
--- stdout ---
▸ Workspace 'child' is stacked on 'ws'. Use becket restack child instead of sync.
