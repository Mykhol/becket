### shell prints the workspace path (by id)

$ becket shell ws
[exit 0]
--- stdout ---
<SANDBOX>/platform/.becket/workspaces/ws

### shell detects the workspace from the current directory

$ becket shell
[exit 0]
--- stdout ---
<SANDBOX>/platform/.becket/workspaces/ws

### shell errors on an unknown workspace

$ becket shell nonexistent
[exit 1]
--- stderr ---
error: Workspace 'nonexistent' not found.
